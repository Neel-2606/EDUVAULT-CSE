About CSE ResourceHub

🧩 The Problem

The CSE Department of MSU Baroda had no central place for academic resources. Syllabi, presentations, notes, previous years' question papers, marks lists and ERC documents were shared through scattered WhatsApp groups and random Google Drive links. Files got buried, links broke, and every year teachers had to forward the same material to the new batch again.

💡 The Solution

CSE ResourceHub is a web-based academic resource management system that consolidates all department study material into a single, organized, year-wise and semester-wise platform covering all 4 years and 8 semesters of the undergraduate program, with role-based access for teachers and students. Material is uploaded once and stays available permanently, so batch changes never require re-sending anything.

✨ Features

  • Two roles: Faculty (full control) and Student (view and download).
  • Year → Semester → Subject → Study Material / PYQs / Other → nested folders → files.
  • Students move to the next year automatically every July, based on their batch.
  • Senior students given permission by faculty can upload previous years' papers for juniors.
  • Upload many files at once with drag & drop and a progress bar.
  • View PDFs and images in the browser, or download any file.
  • Search, sorting, list/grid view and clickable breadcrumbs.
  • Permissions are checked on the server, not just hidden in the browser.

🛠️ Built With

HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, jQuery and AJAX on the front end; Node.js with Express, express-session, multer and bcryptjs on the back end; JSON files as the database.

🚀 Future Scope

  • Announcements and notice board, event updates
  • Placement and internship resources
  • Discussion forum for each subject
  • Email alerts for new uploads and an approval workflow for student uploads
  • Migration to MySQL / MongoDB, an Admin (HOD) role, file version history and download analytics
